Cookie Notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This notice explains how Avanor Solutions uses cookies and similar technologies on avanor.solutions.

Our approach

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this site. The site uses only the limited local storage needed to remember whether you chose to load the optional Calendly scheduler. It is stored in your browser as avanor-scheduler-consent.

Optional scheduler

Calendly is a third-party scheduling service. We do not load its embedded scheduler until you choose "Load the scheduler." Once loaded, Calendly may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies to provide and secure its service. You can avoid this by using the "Open Calendly" link only when you want to schedule, or by contacting us at team@avanor.solutions.

Managing your choice

To withdraw the choice to load the scheduler, clear this site's storage in your browser settings. This does not delete information held by Calendly after you have interacted with its service; use Calendly's controls or contact them for that.

Updates

We will update this notice before adding analytics, advertising tags or other non-essential technologies.
